In short

  • Three classes take new admissions: Nursery, Lower KG and Upper KG.
  • Your child's age on 31 March 2027 decides the class.
  • Dates follow the DoE schedule, which is not out yet.

Quick answers

Will a child in KG this year move to Upper KG in 2027-28?
No. Under the DoE plan, children in KG in 2026-27 move to Class I in 2027-28. Upper KG in 2027-28 takes new admissions only.
Where does a 2026-27 Nursery child go next year?
Children in Nursery in 2026-27 move to Lower KG in 2027-28.
Is a Nursery seat guaranteed after registering?
No. Registering puts your child in the process. It does not guarantee admission.
