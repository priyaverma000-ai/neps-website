Admission forms for open seats in Nursery, Lower KG and Upper KG for the 2027-28 session are available from **1 December 2026**. The last date to submit a form is **28 December 2026**.

## Key dates

- Points of all applicants displayed: 4 January 2027
- Final points displayed: 12 January 2027
- First list, with waiting list: 20 January 2027
- Second list, if any: 5 February 2027
- Admission process closes: 12 March 2027

The registration fee is Rs 25, non-refundable, and buying the prospectus is optional. These dates follow the DoE circular of 20 November 2026.

Read the [full schedule and seats](nursery-admission-2027-28.html), see [how points are given](admission-criteria.html), or use the [age calculator](age-calculator.html) to check which class your child is eligible for.
