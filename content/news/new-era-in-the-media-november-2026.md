New Era Public School, Dwarka featured in several national publications in November 2026.

- **The Hindu, 6 November:** Director Rohit Chopra's article, [Beyond marks: is India ready to skill its schoolchildren?](https://www.thehindu.com/education/beyond-marks-is-india-ready-to-skill-its-schoolchildren/article71669118.ece), on CBSE's revised Class X framework and vocational learning.
- **India Today Insight, 6 November:** Head of School Dr. Priti Ramendra on [how CBSE's vocational option is redefining students' success](https://www.indiatoday.in/india-today-insight/story/with-vocational-option-how-cbse-is-redefining-students-success-3033817-2026-11-06).
- **BW Education, 9 November:** Rohit Chopra on why [wellbeing is not separate from learning](https://www.bweducation.com/article/wellbeing-is-not-separate-from-learning-632701).
- **The Hindu webinar, 14 November:** Rohit Chopra joined a live panel on [career guidance and how to spot children's strengths](https://www.thehindu.com/education/career-guidance-for-children-how-to-spot-their-strengths/article71665268.ece).

The school's story on music, sustainability and experiential learning was also carried by Market Dot News, The 9th Estate, National Biz News and Top News India. See all coverage on [In the news](in-the-news.html).
