Independence Day was celebrated on campus with an NCC parade, patriotic songs from the choir and a nukkad natak.

The day also marked the opening of the school's new **pickleball court**, inaugurated by Chairperson Mrs. Usha Chopra.

Pickleball is now part of the New Era Sports Centre, alongside lawn tennis, shooting, cricket, basketball, football, table tennis, skating, and gym, yoga and Zumba. [See pickleball at New Era](sport-pickleball.html).
