The story

Six decades of New Era

  1. 1960

    New Era begins as a one-room school at Mrs. Usha Chopra's home. She is 23.

  2. 1977

    With land from the Delhi Development Authority, work starts on the school's permanent campus.

  3. 1984

    Education programmes begin for the children of Class IV employees, and later for economically weaker students.

  4. 1989

    A new New Era school opens in Pochanpur, Dwarka, to serve the neighbouring communities.

  5. 1991

    Mrs. Usha Chopra receives the Delhi Government State Award for Excellence in Education.

  6. 1995

    The SPIC MACAY partnership begins, bringing renowned artists to New Era students.

  7. 2025-26

    NCC is introduced at the Dwarka campus.

  8. 2026

    A pickleball court opens on 14 August, the newest part of the Sports Centre.
