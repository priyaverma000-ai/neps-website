Mid-session admission

Moved to Dwarka, or changing schools during the session? Here is how it works.

Call to check a seat
Four steps

How it works

  1. Check for a seatCall or WhatsApp us with your child's class. Mid-session places depend on vacancies.
  2. Visit and meet the teamSee the campus and bring your child's latest report card.
  3. AssessmentFor Class I to VIII, the school's rules base admission on a written and oral assessment.
  4. Documents and feeSchool Leaving Certificate, mark sheet, birth certificate and the admission fee.

Good to know

  • A child who has passed the previous class at a recognised school, with a valid SLC and mark sheet, is exempt from the DoE age criteria for the next class.
  • Monthly fees for 2026-27 range from ₹8,431 to ₹11,650 by class.
  • Fees are due by the 10th of the month. A late fee applies after that.
