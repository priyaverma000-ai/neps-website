Bus safety

  • GPS tracking on every bus.
  • A female attendant on board for every trip.
  • Speed limits kept by every driver.

How to apply

  • Fill in the transport proforma at admission, with your address and nearest stop.
  • The charge is monthly and is paid with the school fee.

Transport rules

  • The transport fee is charged for 11 months of the year and is paid monthly, on time.
  • Transport cannot be stopped in the middle of the session. If it is, the fee is charged for the full session.
  • Apply to start or stop transport at least 30 days before the new session begins.
  • Transport may be withdrawn if the fee is unpaid for more than 15 days.
  • The fee may rise during the session if fuel prices increase or for other reasons.
  • Transport runs on school working days, during school timings. It is a facility, not a right.
  • One-way use is not allowed. The fee is always charged for both ways.
  • To change bus, send a written request with a valid residence proof. It depends on approval and a free seat on that route.
  • No new stops are added to existing routes, and students travel only on their allotted route.
  • Parents cannot ride in the bus with their child.
  • Transport is withdrawn for indiscipline, insubordination towards staff, or damage to a school vehicle.
