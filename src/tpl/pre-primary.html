A day in the nursery wing

  1. Arrival and circle timeGreetings, prayer, a song and a chat about the day.
  2. Language and number playPhonics, stories, counting and shapes, learnt through activities.
  3. Meal togetherThe mid-day meal, eaten in class with the teacher.
  4. Outdoor and movement timeThe play area, yoga and, in summer, the splash pool.
  5. Art, music and danceColouring, scribble boards, rhymes and movement.
  6. Story time and goodbyeA story, a look back at the day and a calm dismissal.

School starts at 7:25 am in summer and 7:45 am in winter. Class sizes and the teacher-child ratio follow DoE norms, and every class has its own mother teacher.
