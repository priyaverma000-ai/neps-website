Our vision

Three words we live by

  • Character

    The moral qualities, ethics and principles that make young people responsible citizens.

  • Commitment

    An unwavering commitment to excellence, the cornerstone of education.

  • Consistency

    Steady progress and growth for every child.

Our mission

To nurture lifelong learners who think critically and communicate effectively.

Our ethos

Values, taught by example

Value education is part of the curriculum. We believe a teacher's attitude shapes how children learn values, so respect, professionalism, humility and kindness set the tone in every classroom.

How we teach

Learning by doing

  • Child-centred, activity-based lessons
  • Real objects, models, Montessori equipment, puppets, films and computers as teaching aids
  • Reasoning, application and critical thinking at every stage
  • Learning linked through art, music, games and competitions
  • A partnership between school and home
Our aims

Growing the whole child

Academic

Learning that is easy and happy, competency-based, and at each child's own pace.

Social

Teamwork with healthy competition, leadership, and care for people, animals and the planet.

Physical

Motor skills, team spirit, fair play and self-discipline through sport, games and treks.

Emotional

Creativity, self-expression, compassion and a positive, confident self-image.

Communication

Spoken English for every child, and inter-school literary competitions.

Values

Humility, work ethic, honesty, duty and legacy.
