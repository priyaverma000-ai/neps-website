School rules

Simple rules that keep school safe, fair and focused.

What time should my child reach school?
By 7:25 am. Students who arrive after that time are not allowed into the school premises.
Can students bring mobile phones?
No. A confiscated phone is returned only when the school issues the child's School Leaving Certificate.
Can students take calls at reception?
No. Students cannot make or receive calls at reception. Urgent messages are passed on to them.
What is expected every day?
Punctuality, regular attendance, proper uniform, work completed on time, and good conduct in and outside school. Students help keep classrooms, corridors and the campus clean.
What behaviour is not allowed?
Insubordination, abusive language, violence, cheating, stealing, bunking, bullying, fighting, smoking, drinking, gambling, obscene material, playing Holi or bursting crackers on campus, and driving a car, scooter or motorbike to school. These invite disciplinary action.
When are fees due?
By the 10th of the month. A late fee is charged after that. Fees can be paid monthly or quarterly.
