School rules

Simple rules that keep school safe, fair and focused.

These rules help every child at New Era Public School, Dwarka learn in a safe, fair and focused school. Please read them with your child at the start of the year.

Rules of conduct and discipline

What time should my child reach school?
By 7:25 am in summer and 7:45 am in winter. Students who arrive after that time are not allowed into the school premises.
Can students bring mobile phones?
No. A phone found with a student is confiscated. Valuables such as smart watches and smart pens are also not allowed; they are confiscated and returned only at the end of the academic year.
Can students take calls at reception?
No. Students cannot make or receive calls at reception. Urgent messages are passed on to them.
What is expected every day?
Punctuality, regular attendance, proper uniform, work completed on time, and good conduct in and outside school. Students keep classrooms, corridors, the building and the campus clean.
What behaviour is not allowed?
Insubordination, abusive language, violence, cheating, stealing, bunking, bullying, fighting, smoking, drinking, gambling, obscene material, playing Holi or bursting crackers on campus, and driving a car, scooter or motorbike to school. These invite disciplinary action.
When are fees due?
By the 10th of the month, paid online through the parent login. A late fine applies after the 10th, and if the fee is not paid by the last day of the month, the student's name is struck off the rolls.

Attendance, tests and leave

How much attendance is needed?
At least 75%, as required by CBSE, for a child to be promoted to the next class.
Is there a re-test if my child misses an exam?
No. There is no re-test for a student who misses a test without a grave reason and the Principal's approval. Such an absence counts as a fail.
How do I apply for leave?
For one day, write in the leave page of the school diary. For a longer absence, send a leave application. For medical leave, attach the medical certificate.
What should my child carry every day?
The school diary (almanac) and identity card, the books for that day's timetable, and a tiffin. Please do not deliver lunch boxes at the gate during school hours.
Which language should students speak at school?
English. Conversing in English is a must at school.

Uniform

What are the uniform rules?
The prescribed school uniform is compulsory, freshly laundered, with polished shoes. A faded, torn or shabby uniform should be replaced. Students must also wear uniform when they come to school for PTMs, events, tests or practice.
What are the grooming rules?
Boys: short, neatly combed hair, with no jewellery, fancy haircuts or beards. Girls: hair tied back or plaited unless very short, with white ribbons only, and no jewellery, long nails, nail polish or make-up.
Can my child wear party clothes on their birthday?
Students of Class I to V may wear party clothes on their birthday. If you send something for classmates, please keep it to one or two eclairs per child, and do not send gifts to school.

Discipline and support

How does the school handle discipline?
A Disciplinary Committee of senior teachers decides corrective measures, which aim only to correct behaviour. The Principal's decision is final. A student who damages school property may be suspended or fined.
Is there a school counsellor?
Yes. The counsellor helps students and parents with academic, emotional and behavioural concerns, on Mondays and Fridays for an hour after the school day. Call the school to book an appointment.

For parents

  • Keep in touch through the school diary, and attend orientation meetings, PTMs and school functions.
  • When you write to the school, give your child's full name, class, section and class teacher.
  • Please do not visit teachers during school hours. For anything urgent, call the school reception.
  • Keep your child at home if they are unwell, and tell the class teacher in writing about any serious medical condition.
  • Make sure your child eats before leaving home.
  • Sign the progress report and send it back within two days.
