Four houses

Rig, Sam, Atharva and Yajur

The four houses are named after the four Vedas, a Sanskrit word for knowledge. Every student belongs to one. Houses compete in sport, music, dance, dramatics, art, public speaking, maths, science, social science and general knowledge.

  • Rig VedaRed · teamwork and diligence
  • Sam VedaBlue · the arts and going beyond
  • Atharva VedaGreen · learning and leading by example
  • Yajur VedaYellow · discipline and clear thinking
  • Student council: elected student leaders for 2026-27
  • Investiture: house and council leaders take their oath each year
