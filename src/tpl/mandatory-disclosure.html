CBSE mandatory public disclosure

The information every CBSE school must publish.

Every CBSE-affiliated school must publish its mandatory public disclosure: general information, documents such as affiliation and recognition certificates, academic results, staff details and infrastructure. New Era Public School, Dwarka publishes it here for parents to check.
