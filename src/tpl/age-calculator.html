The rules

How the DoE sets the age

  • 31 March 2027Your child's age on this date decides the class for 2027-28.
  • One month of relaxationThe Head of School can relax the minimum or maximum age by up to one month.
  • Coming from a recognised schoolA child who passed the previous class, with a valid SLC and mark sheet, is exempt from these age rules for the next class.
