What happens to the details you share with us.

DRAFT: FOR THE SCHOOL'S LEGAL REVIEW

This describes how the website works today. The school should review it, and update it before any form starts saving details or analytics is switched on.

What we ask for

Our enquiry and visit forms ask for your name, mobile number, the class you are interested in and, for visits, a preferred date and time.

How we use it

When you send a form, your details are put into a WhatsApp message to the school's admission team, and you choose whether to send it. We use them only to reply about admissions and visits.

What the website keeps

The website itself does not store form details. The age calculator works in your browser, and the birth date you enter is not sent to us unless you choose to message us.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your details with anyone outside the school.

Questions

Email info@nepsdwarka.com to ask about, correct or delete your details.
