School leadership: the people behind New Era

Experience that spans six decades of New Era, Army Public Schools and leading Delhi schools.

Chairperson

Mrs. Usha Chopra

"We keep the children at the centre of all the teaching, learning and activities conducted at the school."

Her journey in education began in 1960, when New Era started as a one-room school. She has led literacy and community programmes since 1984 and brought SPIC MACAY performances to New Era students since 1995.

Delhi Government State Award for Excellence in Education, 1991Shreshtha Shri AwardUNICEF recognition for literacy work
Director

Mr. Rohit Chopra

"Each child is unique, with different abilities and varied learning needs."

A lifelong sportsperson, he believes sport builds discipline, teamwork and resilience. Under his leadership, New Era students represent the school at State and National level, and the school places weight on communication and learning beyond the classroom.

Head of School

Dr. Priti Ramendra

25 years in teaching and school leadership across Army Public Schools, Delhi Public Schools and leading convent schools. She holds a Ph.D. in English, is an M.Phil. gold medallist and has twice received the Best Teacher Award.

Her focus is strong academic standards, empowered teachers, and learning that is experiential and technology-enabled.

Academic Advisor & Manager

Ms. Kanwal Kohli

Over 35 years in school education, including eight years as Principal of New Era Public School, Dwarka, from 2017 to 2025. She now guides academic planning, curriculum, teacher mentoring and quality.

Best Teacher Award, Lions Club of Delhi, 2013
