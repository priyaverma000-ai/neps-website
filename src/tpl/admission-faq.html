Admission FAQ: your questions answered

Straight answers for 2027-28.

Classes and age

Which classes are open for 2027-28?
Pre-registration is open for Nursery, Lower KG, Upper KG and Class I to IX.
What is the age for Nursery?
Your child should be 3+ and up to 4 years old on 31 March 2027.
What is the age for Lower KG?
Your child should be 4+ and up to 5 years old on 31 March 2027.
What is the age for Upper KG?
Your child should be 5+ and up to 6 years old on 31 March 2027. Upper KG is a new class from 2027-28.
Why are the classes changing?
The Directorate of Education is restructuring the foundational stage in line with NEP 2020, so that children reach Class I at a uniform age of 6+.
My child is in Nursery now. What comes next?
Children in Nursery in 2026-27 move to Lower KG in 2027-28.
My child is in KG now. Do they join Upper KG?
No. Children in KG in 2026-27 move to Class I in 2027-28. Upper KG in 2027-28 takes new admissions only.
Is any age relaxation allowed?
Yes. The Head of School can relax the minimum or maximum age by up to one month, for Nursery to Class I.

Applying

When do forms open?
Dates follow the DoE schedule for 2027-28, which is not out yet. Pre-registration is open now, and we will publish the dates as soon as they are announced.
Does registering guarantee a seat?
No. Registering puts your child in the process. It does not guarantee admission.
How are seats decided?
Seats follow the school's published criteria and the DoE rules. See the Points and criteria page for details.
Can we see the school before we apply?
Yes, and we recommend it. Book a campus visit to see the classrooms, labs and sports facilities and to meet the admission team.
What documents do we need?
Usually the birth certificate, photographs, address proof and identity documents. From Class I upwards, also the School Leaving Certificate and mark sheet. See the documents checklist.
We are moving from another school. Do the age rules apply?
A child who has passed the previous class at a recognised school, with a valid School Leaving Certificate and mark sheet, is exempt from the age criteria for the next higher class.

Fees and daily life

How much are the fees?
In 2026-27 the monthly fee ranges from ₹8,431 to ₹11,650 depending on the class. See the fee structure page for every class.
What does the fee include?
The monthly fee includes A/C charges in every class, a mid-day meal up to Class V, e-learning from Class I to Class X, and STEM and Robotics activities (kits are extra). Uniforms, books, school bags and tours are not included.
What is due at admission?
On the monthly plan, the one-time admission fee of ₹7,196 plus the first month's fee. The school also offers a quarterly plan; see the fee structure page for both.
Are fees refundable?
No. All fees paid are non-refundable and non-transferable.
Is transport available?
Yes. In 2026-27 the charge is ₹3,140 for 0 to 2 km, ₹3,890 for 2 to 5 km and ₹4,280 above 5 km.
Is New Era a CBSE school?
Yes. New Era Public School, Dwarka is a CBSE school, from Nursery to Class XII.
