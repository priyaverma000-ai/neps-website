New for 2027-28

Delhi's school classes are changing

The Directorate of Education has restructured the early years. Here is what it means for your family.

  • 3 classes before Class I

    Nursery, Lower KG and Upper KG, up from Nursery and KG.

  • 6+ for Class I

    The uniform age for Class I is being phased in, reaching 6+ from 2028-29.

  • 31 March 2027

    Your child's age on this date decides the class for 2027-28.
