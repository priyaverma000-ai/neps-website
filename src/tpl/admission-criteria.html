Admission criteria and points 2027-28

A fair process, set out in the open.

First, the age rule

Your child must fit the age for the class on 31 March 2027. Nursery is 3+ up to 4, Lower KG is 4+ up to 5, and Upper KG is 5+ up to 6. The Head of School can relax this by up to one month.

If more children have the same points than there are seats left, the DoE requires a transparent draw of lots, held in front of parents and a DoE nominee and video-recorded. Parents are told the date at least five days before.

At each entry-level class, 25% of seats are reserved for EWS/DG children and children with disabilities, under Section 12(1)(c) of the RTE Act. These seats are filled through a separate DoE process. Class I to IX have 10 seats in each class.
