School FAQ: questions parents ask

About school life at New Era. For admissions, see the Admission FAQ.

The school

Is New Era a CBSE school?
Yes. New Era Public School, Dwarka is a CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school, from Nursery to Class XII.
Where is the school?
In Sector 25, Dwarka, New Delhi 110077, on a 5-acre green campus.
How long has New Era been running?
New Era began in 1960 as a one-room school. The Dwarka campus opened in 1989.
What are the school's values?
Character, Commitment and Consistency. Our mission is to nurture lifelong learners who think critically and communicate effectively.

Learning

Are the classrooms air-conditioned?
Yes. Classrooms are air-conditioned and have interactive smart boards.
What labs does the school have?
Three science labs for physics, chemistry and biology, computer labs, a social science lab, a robotics lab and a high performance computing lab.
Is there support for children who need extra help?
Yes. The school has a full-time counsellor and a full-time special educator, and follows an inclusive curriculum.
Does the school offer NCC?
Yes. NCC was introduced in the 2025-26 session.

Beyond the classroom

Which sports can my child play?
Shooting, lawn tennis, cricket, basketball, football, table tennis, skating, pickleball, gym, yoga and Zumba, with coaches at the New Era Sports Centre.
What clubs are there?
Eleven clubs, including gardening, art and craft, cooking, maths, science, English literary, computer and movie-making, GK and heritage, financial literacy, music and dance.
What are the school houses?
Rig, Sam, Atharva and Yajur. Students compete in sport, music, dance, debate, quizzes and more.

Everyday life

Is lunch provided?
Yes. A mid-day meal is included in the monthly fee up to Class V.
How does the school handle air pollution?
AQI monitors are installed across the campus to track air quality through the day.
Can we visit before applying?
Yes. Book a campus visit online, by WhatsApp or by phone, and our team will show you around.

Still wondering?

Ask us anything. We will call you back.
