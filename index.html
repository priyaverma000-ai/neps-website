Why New Era

Six reasons parents look closer

1960

A legacy that began in one room

New Era began in 1960 as a one-room school and opened in Dwarka in 1989. Over six decades later, the same values guide every classroom.

Sports Centre

Coaches for every sport, on campus.

Lunch included

A mid-day meal in the fee, up to Class V.

Learning you can touch

Science, computer, social science and robotics labs, with smart air-conditioned classrooms.

Caring teachers

A full-time counsellor and special educator.

Safe and green

AQI monitors across a lush campus.
