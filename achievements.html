FAQ

Questions about school achievements

What awards has New Era Public School, Dwarka received?
It is a certified FIT India School and a Swachh Vidyalaya Puraskar recipient. Mrs. Usha Chopra received the Delhi Government State Award for Excellence in Education in 1991.
How many New Era students competed at national level in 2025-26?
Nine students, at CBSE Nationals in lawn tennis, shooting and gymnastics, and at the Khelo India Youth Games.
Does New Era host inter-school events?
Yes, including the Inter-School Sports Meet, the Renaissance and Junior Renaissance festivals, and an education fair.
