Pre-registration open

Class I–IX admission 2027-28

Changing schools is a big step. Here is what to expect, what to bring and who to talk to.

Register Now
Overview Nursery & KG Class I–IX Age calculator Points & criteria Documents Fees FAQ Lists Mid-session

In short

  • Pre-registration is open for Class I to IX.
  • Bring the School Leaving Certificate and mark sheet from the last school.
  • Seats depend on vacancies in each class.

Who can apply

A child who has passed the previous class at a recognised school, with a valid School Leaving Certificate and mark sheet, can move to the next higher class. The DoE exempts these children from the age criteria.

Applying for Class I?

2027-28 is a transition year. In the DoE table, Class I in 2027-28 is filled by children promoted from KG in 2026-27. Our team will tell you what applies to your child, so please speak to us before registering.
